## Featured Project

### Applied Search Intelligence: Google Search Ranking & Discoverability

A Machine Learning research project completed during my FlyRank Machine Learning Internship.

The project explored whether a Decision Tree model could help identify content pages that may benefit from optimization using search-performance signals.

**Model:** Decision Tree Classifier  
**Key features:** GSC Impressions, Average Position, Scroll Events  
**Sample:** 50,000 rows  
**Measured accuracy:** 0.8907  
**Week 4 baseline:** 0.50

The results are observational and intended to support human decision-making rather than automated publishing.
